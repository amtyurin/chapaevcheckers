# Chapaev Checkers: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Chapaev Checkers ("the app") is a game in which you flick checkers to knock the other side's checkers off the board. It is published by Andrey Tyurin.

## Data the app stores

The app saves your game on your device: sound and difficulty settings, the current game (checker positions, scores, wins and shots) and your best scores. This data stays in the app's private storage. The app uses it only to let you continue a game and to show your best scores.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android can include this game data in your device backup to your own Google account. The publisher has no access to that backup.

## Data the app does not collect or share

- The app does not send any data off your device. It has no internet permission.
- The app does not share data with third parties.
- The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries.
- The app does not ask for your name, account, location, contacts or any other personal information.

## Permissions

The app asks for one permission: vibrate. It does not use this permission to collect data.

## Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app, to delete all of its data.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
